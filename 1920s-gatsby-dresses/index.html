Fit notes

Wearing 1920s Styles Above a Size 12

Drop-waist lines should land at the hip, not the widest point.
A slip underneath helps heavy beaded dresses hang smoothly.
Sleeves and cap sleeves are easy to find in 1920s-style reproductions.
Check the dress weight before ordering heavily beaded pieces.
