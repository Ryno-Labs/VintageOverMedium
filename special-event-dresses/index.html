Special Event Dress Fit Tips for Midsize Bodies

Check the bust measurement first on fitted 1950s bodices.
Stretch velvet is forgiving through the bust and hips.
A petticoat adds shape to swing skirts without changing the dress fit.
Always compare your measurements with the brand's own size chart.
