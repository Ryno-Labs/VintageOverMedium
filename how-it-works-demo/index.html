How the affiliate model works

One click shows the whole money flow.

This private demo page uses placeholder products, prices, commission rates, and tracking links to make the business model visible before any affiliate programs are live.

Demo only. Nothing on this page is a forecast, and the product buttons elsewhere on the site do not open the fake affiliate URLs while demo mode is on.

01 — The money path

From Pinterest pin to commission

Pin

A shopper finds a VintageOverMedium pin on Pinterest: “1950s Swing Dresses, Sizes 12–24.”

Page

She lands on the hand-picked edit on vintageovermedium.com.

Click

She taps “Shop at Retro Stage.” Our tracking ID is attached to the link.

Purchase

She buys within the retailer's cookie window — for example, 30 days.

Commission

The retailer pays VintageOverMedium a percentage of the sale. She pays nothing extra.
